學者簡介 / Research Overview

I am an interdisciplinary scholar in ageing and social policy specialising in long-term care, digital inclusion, dementia, and ageing in Chinese communities. I have secured competitive external research grants as Principal Investigator, including GRF and HMRF, and have developed a growing record of peer-reviewed publications in gerontology, social work, and social policy. Drawing on international research experience and cross-sector collaboration, my work connects policy, service delivery, and the lived experiences of older adults and caregivers to inform policymaking and practice innovation.
